# Frequently Asked Questions

## Card Blocking
If you lost your card or suspect fraud:
1. Block your card immediately through the app or by calling us
2. Order a replacement (free for lost cards, $10 for damaged)
3. New physical card ready in 3-5 business days
4. Virtual card issued instantly

## Chargebacks
- Filing deadline: Within 120 days of the transaction
- Processing time: Up to 30 business days
- Required info: date, amount, merchant name, reason

## Limits
- Maximum single transfer: $10,000
- Daily ATM withdrawal: $3,000
- Online purchases: SMS confirmation for amounts over $100

## Security
- Never share your CVV, PIN, or SMS codes with anyone
- The bank will never ask for full card details over the phone
- If you receive a suspicious call, hang up and call our official number: 1-800-NEO-BANK
