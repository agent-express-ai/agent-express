# Plans & Pricing

## Standard Debit Card
- Monthly fee: Free
- Cashback: 1% on all purchases
- ATM withdrawals: Free at our ATMs, 1.5% at third-party ATMs
- Transfer limit: $5,000/month

## Premium Debit Card
- Monthly fee: $9.99/month (free with $10,000+ balance)
- Cashback: 3% in selected categories, 1% on everything else
- ATM withdrawals: Free everywhere up to $3,000/month
- Transfer limit: $30,000/month
- Free airport lounge access

## Credit Card
- Grace period: Up to 55 days
- APR: From 14.9%
- Credit limit: Up to $25,000
- Minimum payment: 3% of balance or $25, whichever is greater

## Transfers
- Between own accounts: Free, instant
- Domestic (other banks): Free up to $5,000/month
- Wire transfer: $15 per transfer
- International (SWIFT): From 0.15%, minimum $15
